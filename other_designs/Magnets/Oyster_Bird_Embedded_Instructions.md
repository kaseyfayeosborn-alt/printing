# Oyster bird magnet — completely hidden 10 × 2 mm magnet

Use **07_Oyster_Bird_Embedded_10x2_PETG.3mf** in desktop Creality Print. Open it **as a project** to retain its four colors, PETG presets and insertion pause.

The hanging loop is removed. The detailed shell and bird are preserved, and the outside back is fully closed and flat. Overall size: approximately 51.66 × 76.20 × 11.71 mm (3 inches tall).

## Insert the magnet

1. Keep the supplied size and the saved 0.12 mm layer / 0.20 mm first-layer settings. Resizing also changes the magnet cavity.
2. Load PETG in CFS order: **1 White, 2 Gray, 3 Light Blue, 4 Navy**. Map the project colors to those spools, slice, and retain the pause marker at **3.08 mm**.
3. When the printer pauses, place **one 10 mm diameter × 2 mm thick magnet** in the visible round cavity. Seat it fully below the printed edge and keep it seated as printing resumes. Use a heat-rated magnet suitable for embedding during PETG printing.
4. Resume on the printer. The covering layers begin at **3.20 mm**, sealing the magnet inside.

The cavity is 10.3 mm in diameter with 2.28 mm of vertical space. It starts 0.8 mm above the back, so no magnet or hole is visible on the outside. The magnet is beneath the bird's body, with more than 3 mm of modeled plastic above the completed cavity.

The **STL contains geometry only**. It does not store colors or the pause; add the same pause manually if you use the STL. The 3MF is the recommended printing file.

## Checks

Both files imported and sliced in Creality Print 7.3.1.6378 without warnings. The generated toolpath contains exactly one PAUSE, with the cavity open at insertion and the closed rear floor already printed. The geometry is watertight with an intentionally enclosed internal void. Physical printing and a physical pause/resume have not been tested.
